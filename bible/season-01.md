## Episodes 2 onward

Not yet written. Working outline:

- **The fracture** (late February to early March): the first night in the basement; the campus sealed as a containment zone; the state's first visible response; families on the phone; Kabir's family a short drive away.
- **The long hold** (March to April): rules, rationing, other survivors, screening and lists, a bitten friend; the infected moving into cool places; the heat effect discovered; "hold until May."
- **The heat** (May to the monsoon): the hold working on the plains, the infected concentrated in the last cool places, the external threat, and the first rain.
